## 🎯 The Fundamental Problem: The Coupon Dilemma

Imagine an e-commerce retailer that sent 20% discount coupons to customers who logged in frequently.
- Sales among coupon recipients averaged **$150**.
- Sales among non-recipients averaged **$80**.

**The million-dollar question:** Did the coupon *cause* a $70 lift, or were those customers already primed to spend more?

In observational data, correlation is not causation. Customers who received the coupon differed systematically from those who did not (**selection bias** and **confounding**). Deploying policies based on naive observational comparisons often results in massive financial losses.

**Off-Policy Evaluation (OPE)** bridges this gap: it allows data scientists and product leaders to estimate the counterfactual performance of a new proposed decision policy $\pi_e$ using only historical interaction logs generated by a previous logging policy $\pi_0$—without executing risky, expensive live A/B tests.

## 🌐 What OPE Lab Does (A to Z)

**OPE Lab** is a complete, interactive, mission-control laboratory:

1. **Simulates Ground Truth Contextual Bandit Environments:**
   - Generates observational interactions with user context $X$, unobserved confounder $U$, logged decisions $A$, and observed rewards $R$.
   - Features controllable hidden confounding strength $c \in [0, 1]$ and logging policy overlap (positivity) $L \in [0.05, 1]$.

2. **Evaluates 6 Distinct Statistical Estimators:**
   - Evaluates **Naive Observational**, **Replay**, **Direct Method (DM)**, **Inverse Propensity Weighting (IPW)**, **Self-Normalized IPW (SNIPW)**, and **Doubly Robust (DR)** side-by-side against the analytical interventional truth.

3. **Weight Health Diagnostics:**
   - Computes Kish's Effective Sample Size ($\text{ESS}$) and maximum importance weight ($\max w_i$), warning practitioners when importance weights explode or collapse.

4. **Quantifies Uncertainty with Bootstrap:**
   - Computes 95% Confidence Intervals and standard errors using an adaptive percentile bootstrap.

5. **Marginal Sensitivity Analysis:**
   - Computes worst-case reward bounds under potential unobserved confounding using the sorted-reward threshold sweep over $[w/\Lambda, w \cdot \Lambda]$.
   - Calculates **$\Lambda^*$**: The minimum unobserved confounding required to explain away the policy's estimated value.

6. **Failure Study (Empirical Monte Carlo Sweeps):**
   - **Chart A (Confounding Sweep):** Evaluates all 6 estimators as hidden confounding $c$ scales from 0.0 to 1.0 (30 seeds/level, $n=5,000$). Features an interactive toggle between Absolute Bias and RMSE, a 0.05 tolerance threshold line, and real-time plain-English takeaways.
   - **Chart B (Overlap Breakdown):** Tests overlap decay $\alpha \in [0.1, 1.0]$ at $c=0$, contrasting estimator RMSE alongside median Kish Effective Sample Size ($\text{ESS}$) to reveal IPW variance explosion.

7. **Estimator Decision Map (3×3 Empirical Phase Diagram):**
   - 9-cell grid mapping Confounding $c \in \{0.0, 0.5, 1.0\} \times$ Overlap $\alpha \in \{0.2, 0.5, 0.9\}$ with $\ge 20$ replications per cell ($n=5,000$).
   - **Winner Selection:** Strict lowest RMSE (Naive excluded).
   - **Tie Detection:** Cells where the top two estimators are within 10% RMSE are marked **Tie**.
   - **Mandatory Honesty Rule:** When the lowest-RMSE estimator has $|Bias| > 0.05$, the cell is marked **"No method reliable"** (muted slate) — upholding causal integrity because hidden confounding invalidates unadjusted OPE methods.
   - **Cell Inspector:** Interactive dropdown to inspect exact Mean, Bias, Abs Bias, Variance, Std Error, and RMSE across all 6 estimators for any parameter combination.

8. **Automated Evidence & Facts Generator:**
   - Standalone CLI pipeline (`scripts/make_abstract_facts.py`) executing multi-seed Monte Carlo benchmarks and adaptive bootstrap inference. Outputs exact, verifiable empirical numbers directly to `docs/abstract_facts.md` (no fabricated data).

## 📐 Causal Ground Truth: Interventional Value

The simulator implements a known causal Data Generating Process (DGP):
- Context: $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$
- Hidden Confounder: $U \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$
- Logging Policy:
  $$\pi_0(A=1 \mid X, U) = \sigma(L \cdot X + 3c \cdot U)$$
  where $L = 0.5 + 5(1 - \text{overlap})$ controls propensity positivity.
- Structural Reward Equation:
  $$R = 0.5 + 0.3X + A(0.2 + 0.2X + 0.5U) + 0.4U + \sigma_{\text{noise}} \epsilon$$
  *(The interaction term $0.5 A \cdot U$ ensures that hidden confounder $U$ modifies the treatment effect directly, exposing estimators to genuine omitted-variable bias in observational logged data).*
- New Target Policy to Evaluate ($\pi_e$):
  Treat ($A=1$) if $X > 0$, else do not treat ($A=0$).

### Analytical Interventional Value:
Under Pearl's causal intervention $\text{do}(A = \pi_e(X))$:
$$V^*(\pi_e) = \mathbb{E}[R \mid \text{do}(A = \pi_e(X))] = 0.5 + 0.3\,\mathbb{E}[X] + 0.2\,\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{I}(X > 0)] + 0.2\,\mathbb{E}[X \cdot \mathbb{I}(X > 0)] + 0.5\,\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{I}(X > 0) \cdot U] + 0.4\,\mathbb{E}[U]$$
Since $\mathbb{E}[X]=0$, $\mathbb{E}[U]=0$, $U \perp X \implies \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{I}(X > 0) \cdot U] = 0$, $\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{I}(X>0)]=0.5$, and $\mathbb{E}[X \cdot \mathbb{I}(X>0)] = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \approx 0.398942$:
$$V^*(\pi_e) = 0.5 + 0.1 + 0.2 \times 0.398942 = 0.679788$$

Estimators **never observe $U$**, reflecting the real-world challenge where unmeasured confounding distorts propensity scores.

## 📊 The 6 Estimators & Diagnostics

| Estimator | Formula | Key Strength | Vulnerability / Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Naive** | $\frac{1}{N_1}\sum_{i: A_i=1} R_i$ | Simple benchmark | Completely blind to selection bias and confounding |
| **Replay** | $\frac{\sum_{i: A_i=\pi_e(X_i)} R_i}{\sum_{i: A_i=\pi_e(X_i)} 1}$ | Unweighted matching | Biased if logging policy was non-uniform or confounded |
| **Direct Method (DM)** | $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \hat{f}(X_i, \pi_e(X_i))$ | Low variance | Misspecification and omitted confounder bias |
| **IPW** | $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n w_i R_i$ | Unbiased under positivity & $c=0$ | High variance; weights explode with low overlap |
| **Self-Normalized IPW** | $\frac{\sum w_i R_i}{\sum w_i}$ | Bounded predictions; lower variance | Finite-sample bias; breaks under hidden confounding |
| **Doubly Robust (DR)** | $\frac{1}{n}\sum [ \hat{r}_i(\pi_e) + w_i(R_i - \hat{r}_i(A_i)) ]$ | Consistent if either model is correct | Most resilient OPE method; degrades gracefully |

### Diagnostics:
- **Kish's Effective Sample Size ($\text{ESS}$):** $\frac{(\sum w_i)^2}{\sum w_i^2}$
- **Status Chips:**
  - 🟢 **Healthy:** $\text{ESS} / N \ge 30\%$
  - 🟡 **Caution:** $10\% \le \text{ESS} / N < 30\%$
  - 🔴 **Unreliable:** $\text{ESS} / N < 10\%$

## ⚡ Quickstart & Installation

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/mailtojaswanthss-svg/ope-lab.git
cd ope-lab

# Create Python 3.11 virtual environment
py -3.11 -m venv .venv

# Activate virtual environment
# Windows PowerShell:
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
# Linux / macOS:
source .venv/bin/activate

# Install dependencies
pip install -r requirements.txt

# Launch OPE Lab
streamlit run app.py
```
Open your browser at `http://localhost:8501`.

## 🎬 How to Demo in 90 Seconds

1. **Step 1: The Intuition (15s)**
   - Click the **📖 Overview** tab.
   - Point to the **Coupon Story** and explain: "If high-spenders always got coupons in historical logs, comparing coupon recipients to non-recipients is fatally biased."
2. **Step 2: The Ideal RCT Baseline (15s)**
   - Click the sidebar button **`Easy RCT`** ($c=0.0, \text{overlap}=0.9$).
   - Switch to the **📊 Results** tab.
   - Show how **Doubly Robust (DR)** and **IPW** align closely with the cyan dashed Ground Truth $V^* = 0.6798$ with tight 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.
3. **Step 3: The Low Overlap Stress Test (15s)**
   - Click the sidebar button **`Low Ovl`** ($c=0.0, \text{overlap}=0.15$).
   - Point to the weight histogram: importance weights spike, and standard IPW's error bars explode.
   - Show how **SNIPW** and **DR** remain stable and controlled.
4. **Step 4: The Confounding Trap (15s)**
   - Click the sidebar button **`Trap`** ($c=0.8, \text{overlap}=0.7$).
   - Show the prominent orange **⚠️ Unobserved Confounding Active** banner:
     *"Doubly Robust protects against model misspecification under unconfoundedness, but CANNOT fix hidden confounding."*
   - Show that all estimators exhibit positive bias, proving why sensitivity bounds are mandatory before deployment.
5. **Step 5: Failure Study Sweeps (15s)**
   - Switch to the **💥 Failure Study** tab.
   - Toggle between **Absolute Bias** and **RMSE** to show how error scales with hidden confounding $c$.
   - Highlight the 0.05 red tolerance threshold line and the dynamic takeaway explaining which estimators fail first.
6. **Step 6: Estimator Decision Map (15s)**
   - Switch to the **🗺️ Decision Map** tab.
   - Point to the 3×3 phase matrix: show where **Doubly Robust / SNIPW** win, where **Ties** occur, and the gray **"No Method Reliable"** zone where hidden confounding exceeds tolerance.
   - Use the **Cell Inspector** dropdowns to view exact empirical metrics across all 6 estimators.
